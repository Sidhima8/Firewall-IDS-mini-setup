# Nmap Firewall Verification

## Objective

To verify the configured firewall rules and check the accessibility of network services using Nmap.

## Testing

Nmap scans were performed after configuring the firewall.

## Results

The scan confirmed:
- Port 80 – Open
- Blocked ports – Filtered

## Conclusion

The Nmap scan successfully verified that the firewall was allowing the required HTTP service while blocked ports were filtered.
